# RentRewards

**Renting, on the record.**

RentRewards is a rental platform that connects the people involved in a tenancy —
renters, home seekers, property owners, managers, service providers and partner
businesses — around one shared, verified record of what actually happened.

## Why we're building it

Renting generates a long history of meaningful behaviour: rent paid on time,
repairs reported and resolved, tenancies completed in good standing. Almost none
of it is recorded in a way that benefits the people involved. Renters start from
zero every time they move. Owners and managers work from incomplete information.
Good tenants and reliable service providers get no credit for it.

We think that record should exist, belong to the people who create it, and be
worth something.

## What the platform does

- **Verified rental records** — payments, tenancies and maintenance captured as
  evidence, not recollection.
- **Transparent payments** — a clear ledger for rent, with instant proof for both
  sides.
- **Rewards for reliability** — on-time rent earns benefits renters can actually
  use.
- **A portable rental reputation** — renters choose what to share, with whom, for
  what purpose and for how long.
- **Coordinated operations** — listings, applications, maintenance and reporting
  in one place for owners, managers and contractors.

## Principles

- **Consent first.** Personal data moves only when the person it belongs to says so.
- **Explainable by default.** Anything that affects someone should be something
  they can see, understand and challenge.
- **Evidence over assumption.** Decisions rest on records, not guesswork.
